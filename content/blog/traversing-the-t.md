Recently railfanned The T while I was at PAX East 2024! Got a chance to get a shot of all lines except for silver since it's a bus line. Overall, I didn't feel very safe railfanning in certain stations late at night (Downtown Crossing & State to name a few), and there were also a lot of delays the weekend I went. The Orange line was even suspended between Jackson Sq. and North Station the first night I went! Ridiculous! One of the only redeeming factors of my trip was that the Green Line operators were super nice when they saw me filming.
